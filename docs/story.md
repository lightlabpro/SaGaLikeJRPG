# Story Bible — *SaGaLikeJRPG* (working title: **Coil of Embers**)

> **Status:** Draft for approval. No area art or maps until this + the world map are signed off.  
> **Targets:** SaGa Frontier 2 structure (~25–30 hours, scenario-driven, non-linear) + Final Fantasy VII tone (futuristic **steampunk industrial**; cyberpunk neon toned down).  
> **Visuals:** SF2 handpainted watercolor technique applied to brass, iron, steam, smog, and megacity ruins — not medieval fantasy.

---

## One-line pitch

Two lives — a magicless steel-swordsman cast out of the corporate throne, and a scrapland excavator chasing a buried “Egg” — carve overlapping histories across a world powered by stolen planetary anima, until their paths decide whether the Coil burns out or reignites.

---

## Tone & influences

| Borrow from SF2 | Borrow from FF7 | Our blend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scenario select / multi-arc story | Mega-corp dystopia, eco-horror | Non-linear chapters inside one industrial planet-city ring |
| “No anima” steel protagonist | Midgar-like stratified city | **Gustave-class** heir who cannot sync to **Volt** |
| Excavator / digger saga | Ancient weapons, reactors, Sephiroth-scale threat | Diggers unearth a sealed **Kernel** (the Egg) |
| Soft watercolor screens | Reactors, airships, undercity, corporate dystopia | Watercolor brass-smog industrial — neon rare |

Not a FF7 remake. Original cast, places, and metaphysics — same *feel*.

## Core metaphysics (keep simple)

- **Volt** — living planetary current (lifestream / mako analogue). Visible as pale cyan–amber in pipes and rain.
- **Helix Conglomerate** — monopoly that drills, refines, and sells Volt. Owns law, media, and the sky-fleet.
- **Sync** — the ability to weave Volt into arts (magic). Most combatants sync; a few cannot.
- **Steel** — anti-Volt craft. Weapons and frames that refuse the current. Feared, banned in polite wards.
- **The Kernel** — a sealed pre-Helix engine-heart under the Coil. Helix calls it myth. Diggers call it the Egg.

Theme triangle: **inheritance / depletion / choice** — who owns the planet’s blood, and what you become when you take it.

---

## Dual protagonists

### Arc A — **Ash Rennet** (the Steel Heir)

- Exiled scion of Helix’s founding house. Born **unable to sync** — a political scandal.
- Fights with steel blades and scavenged frames; learns arts through grit and glimmer-like breakthroughs that are *physical*, not Volt.
- Emotional spine: prove worth without the gift everyone worships; decide whether to burn Helix down or remake it.
- SF2 echo: Gustave. FF7 echo: Cloud’s “not who they said I was,” plus Barret’s corporate fury without copying either.

### Arc B — **Nyx Calder** (the Excavator)

- Ruin-diver from the Rust Marches; crew digs pre-Helix vaults for salvage.
- Discovers a Kernel fragment that dreams in their sleep — and Helix wants it.
- Emotional spine: family/crew loyalty vs. the pull of something older than the Conglomerate.
- SF2 echo: Wil Knights. FF7 echo: Avalanche’s underdog dig into the machine.

The arcs **interleave**. Some chapters are Ash-only, some Nyx-only, some shared. Scenario select (SF2-style) unlocks after the prologue; late game forces convergence.

## Scenario structure (~25–30 hours)

Aim: **~12–16 playable scenarios** + free roam digressions, totaling **25–30 hours** for a completionist first run (critical path ~18–22h).

### Prologue (fixed, ~1.5h)

1. **Ash** — exile from **Lumen Spire**; first steel fight in the undercity.
2. **Nyx** — dig goes wrong in **Rust Marches**; Kernel shard found.

Unlock **Scenario Board** (SF2 event map energy).

### Early Coil (player picks order, ~8–10h)

| ID | Scenario | Lead | Region | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | *Smoke Rights* | Ash | Cinder Wards | Labor revolt; first Helix crackdown |
| E2 | *Deep Salvage* | Nyx | Rust Marches | Crew debt; Pale Choir ambush |
| E3 | *Neon Baptism* | Ash | Glassmere | Smuggler job; Quill joins |
| E4 | *Skyhook Debt* | Either | Skyhook Docks | Iri’s ship impounded |
| E5 | *The Soft Ward* | Ash | Lumen Spire outskirts | Meet Vesper in secret |
| E6 | *Vault Mice* | Nyx | Pale Grid fringe | First corporate server-ruin |

### Mid Coil (gates open after ~4 early clears, ~8–10h)

| ID | Scenario | Lead | Region | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | *Reactor Hymn* | Ash | Aetherwell Rim | Sabotage vs. save civilians |
| M2 | *Egg Dream* | Nyx | Deep Marches | Kernel vision; Rook’s fate |
| M3 | *Sol’s Parade* | Ash | Lumen Spire | Public duel with Sol Kade |
| M4 | *Black Ledger* | Nyx | Chromehaven Undercity | Proof Helix knew about Kernel |
| M5 | *Cinder Crown* | Either | Cinder Reaches | Warlord forge-city alliance |
| M6 | *Ghost Frequency* | Shared | Pale Grid | Choir + Helix both hunting Echo |

### Late Coil (convergence, ~6–8h)

| ID | Scenario | Lead | Region | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | *Two Shadows* | Shared | Anywhere → Spire | Ash & Nyx finally party together |
| L2 | *Boardroom Blood* | Ash | Helix Core | Coup / expose |
| L3 | *Heart of the Coil* | Shared | Aetherwell Depths | Enter the Kernel chamber |
| L4 | *Ember Ending* | Shared | Kernel / Spire | Branching finale |

### Endings (3 primary)

1. **Severance** — shut the drills; hard dark age, free planet.
2. **Stewardship** — redistribute Volt under new compact; fragile peace.
3. **Ascension** — Kernel merges with a protagonist; ambiguous transcendence (SaGa weirdness welcome).
